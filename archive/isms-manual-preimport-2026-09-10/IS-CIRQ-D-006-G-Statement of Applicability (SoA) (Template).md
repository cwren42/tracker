**1. Purpose**

The Statement of Applicability (SoA) documents the information security
controls selected by Cirque for implementation within its Information
Security Management System (ISMS) based on the results of the risk
assessment and treatment process. It also justifies the inclusion or
exclusion of controls from ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, aligning with
Clause 6.1.3 d) and 6.1.3 e).

**2. Scope**

This SoA covers all controls within the Cirque ISMS scope as defined in
IS-CIRQ-D-001-G: ISMS Scope Document.

**3. Instructions for Use**

-   This document is created and maintained by the IT Manager.

-   It should be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur to
    the ISMS scope, risk assessment results, or business context.

-   For each Annex A control, specify if it is included and the
    justification, or if it is excluded and the justification.
