# SOC2 Incident Response Plan

**Document ID:** SOC2-IRP-001  
**Owner:** IT/Security Lead  
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-02  
**Review Frequency:** Annual + after major incident

## 1. Purpose
Provide a repeatable response process for security incidents to contain impact, preserve evidence, and meet legal and customer obligations.

## 2. Scope
Applies to incidents affecting confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy, or compliance.

## 3. Incident Severity
- **SEV-1 Critical:** Service outage/data breach/active compromise
- **SEV-2 High:** Material security event with business impact
- **SEV-3 Medium:** Contained event requiring remediation
- **SEV-4 Low:** Informational event

## 4. Response Lifecycle
1. **Detect and Triage**
2. **Contain**
3. **Investigate and Eradicate**
4. **Recover and Validate**
5. **Communicate and Report**
6. **Post-Incident Review and CAPA**

## 5. Time Objectives
- Initial triage: within 30 minutes for SEV-1/SEV-2
- Containment start: within 2 hours for SEV-1
- Stakeholder update cadence: every 2 hours (SEV-1) until stabilized

## 6. Required Artifacts
- Incident ticket and timeline
- Indicators of compromise and affected assets
- Containment and recovery actions
- Notification decisions and approvals
- Lessons learned and corrective actions

## 7. SOC 2 Evidence
- Incident register
- Response drill records
- Post-incident reports
- Corrective action tracking
