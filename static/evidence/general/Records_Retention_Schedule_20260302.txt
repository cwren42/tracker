Purpose:
--------
This Records Retention Schedule defines the minimum and maximum retention
periods for all business records and data maintained by Cirque Corporation.
These retention periods comply with legal, regulatory, and business requirements.

Scope:
------
This schedule applies to all records in all formats including:
- Electronic records (databases, files, emails, cloud storage)
- Paper records (documents, forms, agreements)
- Audio/video recordings (meetings, training, surveillance)
- Backup copies and archives

Legal Basis:
------------
- Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX) - Financial records
- Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) - Employment records
- HIPAA - Health-related records
- GDPR - Personal data of EU residents
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - California resident data
- IRS regulations - Tax and financial records
- State employment laws - Personnel records

DISPOSAL METHODS DEFINED
================================================================================

SECURE SHRED (Paper Documents):
- Cross-cut shredding to DIN P-4 standard or higher
- Shredded material: < 1/16" x 5/8" particles
- Performed by: IT staff (internal) or Iron Mountain (external)
- Certificate of Destruction: Required for all confidential documents
- Verification: Photo/video evidence required

SECURE DELETE (Electronic Files):
- Single File Deletion: DoD 5220.22-M 3-pass overwrite minimum
- Bulk Deletion: NIST 800-88 Clear or Purge methods
- Tools: DBAN, Eraser, BitRaser, or built-in secure delete
- Verification: Deletion log with file count, date, operator
- Cloud Data: Request deletion from provider + verify

CRYPTOGRAPHIC ERASE (Encrypted Storage):
- Destroy encryption keys, rendering data unrecoverable
- Applicable to: BitLocker volumes, encrypted databases, encrypted backups
- Verification: Key destruction logged
- Faster than overwriting for large volumes

PHYSICAL DESTRUCTION (Storage Media):
- Hard Drives: Degaussing + physical shredding/crushing
- SSDs: Cryptographic erase + physical destruction (no magnetic method)
- USB Drives: Physical destruction
- Backup Tapes: Degaussing + physical shredding
- CDs/DVDs: Physical shredding
- Mobile Devices: Factory reset + physical destruction
- Provider: Iron Mountain or certified e-waste recycler
- Certificate of Destruction: REQUIRED (with serial numbers)

OVERWRITE/DELETE (Video/Logs):
- Standard deletion (non-confidential data)
- Overwrite in normal course of business (circular logs)
- No recovery attempt expected

RETENTION SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

11.1 SCHEDULE REVIEW
---------------------
✓ Annual review: March (every year)
✓ Review participants: IT Manager, CFO, Legal Counsel, CTO
✓ Updates triggered by:
  - Changes in applicable laws or regulations
  - New business processes or data types
  - Audit findings or recommendations
  - Industry best practice updates

11.2 LEGAL HOLDS
----------------
When litigation, investigation, or audit is anticipated or in progress:
✓ IT Manager or Legal Counsel issues "Legal Hold" notification
✓ All records related to matter are preserved indefinitely
✓ Automated deletion processes suspended for affected records
✓ Legal Hold remains until explicitly released by Legal Counsel
✓ Records under Legal Hold: Not subject to normal retention schedule

11.3 DISPOSAL TRACKING
----------------------
✓ All disposals logged in IT ticketing system
✓ Disposal record includes:
  - Date of disposal
  - Type and volume of records destroyed
  - Method of destruction
  - Person responsible for disposal
  - Certificate of Destruction (if external vendor)
✓ Disposal logs retained for 7 years

11.4 EXCEPTIONS
---------------
Exception requests must be submitted to:
- IT Manager (for IT systems and data)
- CFO (for financial records)
- Legal Counsel (for legal/compliance records)

Approved exceptions documented with:
- Business justification
- Duration of exception
- Compensating controls
- Annual re-approval requirement

11.5 EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
-------------------------------
✓ All employees must comply with this Records Retention Schedule
✓ Employees may not:
  - Retain records beyond scheduled disposal date (except Legal Hold)
  - Delete records before minimum retention period
  - Remove records from company custody without authorization
✓ Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination
✓ Training: New hire orientation + annual refresher
