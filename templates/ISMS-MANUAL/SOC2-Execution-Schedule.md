# SOC 2 Weekly Execution Schedule (from Strike Graph exports)

## Week 1
- Finalize SOC 2 scope (Security-only vs Security+Availability/Confidentiality/Privacy/PI).
- Assign owners to unassigned controls and risks.
- Close all template/instruction text in risks and controls.
- Publish control evidence standard (artifact, reviewer, retention).

## Week 2
- Implement governance + HR controls (Code of Conduct, Background Checks, Job Descriptions, Oversight cadence).
- Implement foundational policy controls (Logical Access, Data Classification, Data Management, Vendor Management).
- Finalize and approve System Description.

## Week 3
- Implement access controls (provisioning, deprovisioning SLA, access review, password/auth, privileged access).
- Stand up change management workflow (ticketing, approvals, emergency changes, SoD).
- Capture first execution evidence for each new control.

## Week 4
- Implement security operations controls (monitoring, intrusion detection, vulnerability scans, firewall governance, encryption evidence).
- Execute incident response tabletop and retain outputs.
- Complete risk scoring and treatment entries for all active risks.

## Week 5
- Execute vendor lifecycle controls (due diligence, SOC report review, annual vendor review, vendor risk register updates).
- Complete BCP/DR test and remediation actions.
- Run internal readiness audit sample (top 15 controls by risk impact).

## Week 6
- Close findings via CARs and residual risk approvals.
- Verify every active control has period-appropriate evidence.
- Final management review focused on SOC 2 readiness and go/no-go for audit window.
