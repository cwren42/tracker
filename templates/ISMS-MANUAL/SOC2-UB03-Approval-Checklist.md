# UB-03 Risk Scoring Approval Checklist

## Objective
Approve first-pass scoring for risks so `UB-03` can be marked complete.

## Inputs
- `SOC2-Risk-Cleanup-Register.csv`
- `SOC2-Risk-Scoring-Draft.csv`

## Review steps
1. Validate scoring for the **31 scored** risks in `SOC2-Risk-Scoring-Draft.csv`.
2. For the **7 TBD risks**, wait for `UB-04` scope decisions, then finalize score.
3. Confirm the final risk scoring convention is Low/Medium/High and consistent with ISMS policy.
4. Record reviewer approval (name/date) and update Strike Graph risk records.

## Completion criteria for UB-03
- All non-scope-pending risks scored and approved.
- Scoring rationale retained.
- Strike Graph risks updated with approved scores.
