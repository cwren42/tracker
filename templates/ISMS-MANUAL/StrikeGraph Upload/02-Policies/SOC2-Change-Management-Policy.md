# SOC2 Change Management Policy

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-CHG-001  
**Owner:** IT/Security Lead  
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-02  
**Review Frequency:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
Ensure system and configuration changes are authorized, tested, documented, and approved before production release.

## 2. Scope
Applies to infrastructure, applications, security tooling, access configurations, and production data-impacting changes.

## 3. Change Categories
- **Standard:** Pre-approved repeatable changes.
- **Normal:** Planned changes requiring risk, testing, and approval.
- **Emergency:** Time-sensitive changes requiring expedited approval and post-change review.

## 4. Mandatory Control Requirements
- Unique change ticket for every production-impacting change.
- Risk assessment and rollback plan before approval.
- Segregation of duties between requester and approver.
- Testing evidence attached prior to release.
- Post-implementation validation and closure notes.

## 5. Emergency Changes
- Require on-call manager approval.
- Must include business/security justification.
- Reviewed within 2 business days after implementation.

## 6. Roles
- **Requester:** Defines scope, risk, and implementation plan.
- **Approver:** Validates risk, testing, and timing.
- **Implementer:** Executes approved change.
- **IT/Security:** Performs oversight and periodic quality review.

## 7. Evidence for SOC 2
- Change tickets
- Approval records
- Test results and rollback plans
- Emergency change post-review records
