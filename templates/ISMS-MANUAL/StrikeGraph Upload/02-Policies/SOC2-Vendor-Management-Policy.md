# SOC2 Vendor Management Policy

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-VM-001  
**Owner:** IT/Security Lead  
**Effective Date:** 2026-03-02  
**Review Frequency:** Annual

## 1. Purpose
Define due diligence and ongoing oversight for third parties that process, host, or access Cirque systems or data.

## 2. Scope
Applies to vendors, service providers, contractors, and strategic partners with technical access or data handling responsibilities.

## 3. Required Vendor Lifecycle Controls
- Security and privacy due diligence before onboarding.
- Contract terms must include confidentiality and security obligations.
- Risk tier assignment (High/Medium/Low).
- Annual review for critical vendors.
- Documented offboarding and access revocation.

## 4. Minimum Security Requirements for High-Risk Vendors
- Current SOC 2 report or equivalent assurance
- Incident notification obligations
- Access controls and least privilege
- Data encryption in transit and at rest
- Subprocessor disclosure (if applicable)

## 5. Roles
- **Vendor Owner:** Business accountability for vendor relationship.
- **IT/Security:** Security due diligence and periodic reassessment.
- **Procurement/Legal:** Contract controls and legal review.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions require risk acceptance with defined compensating controls and executive approval.

## 7. Evidence for SOC 2
- Vendor inventory and risk tiering
- Due diligence questionnaires
- Contracts/SOWs
- SOC reports/certifications
- Annual review records
