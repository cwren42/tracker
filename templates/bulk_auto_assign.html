How Auto-Assignment Works

This feature automatically assigns assets to employees by matching employee names found in asset names.

Examples:

  • AARON-XPS → Matches employee "Aaron Smith"
  • CHRIS-LENOVO → Matches employee "Chris Wren"
  • MIKE-MSI → Matches employee "Mike Layton" (or first "Mike" found)
Note: Only unassigned assets will be processed. Assets already assigned to employees will not be changed.
