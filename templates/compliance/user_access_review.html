Scope, sources and limitations

Sources. Account listing from Microsoft Entra ID (m365_user, current sync). Privileged access from the Entra directory-role snapshot (admin_role_snapshot) dated {{ snapshot_day or 'n/a' }}. Employee linkage from the HR/AD employee record.

Population scope — Cirque accounts only. The review covers the {{ stats.total_accounts }} accounts on the @cirque.com domain that belong to Cirque personnel. The tenant also contains {{ stats.guests_excluded }} external B2B guest accounts invited from customer and supplier organisations (provisioned as …#EXT#@…), plus accounts on the default @cirque1.onmicrosoft.com domain. Those parties are affiliated with Cirque but are not Cirque personnel: they hold no internal system access and are deliberately out of scope for this review of internal logical access. Guest collaboration access is governed separately through the tenant's external-sharing controls. This exclusion is stated so the in-scope population reconciles to the {{ stats.tenant_total }}-account tenant listing.

Why one population covers four layers. Entra ID is the single identity provider in front of the operating system, database, application and network/cloud layers, so the account listing below governs access to all four. No separate per-layer access level is asserted.

Not assessed in this report. Sign-in recency and license assignment are not collected in the current Entra sync (last_signin_datetime and licenses are empty for every account), so account inactivity has not been evaluated here. Assessing dormant accounts requires enabling signInActivity collection (Graph AuditLog.Read.All).
