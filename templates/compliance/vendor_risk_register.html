Risk Assessment Criteria

Risk Level Definitions
  • Low: SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certified, established vendor, minimal data access
  • Medium: Some compliance certifications, moderate data access
  • High: No certifications, high data access, new vendor
Criticality Levels
  • Critical: Service outage would halt business operations
  • High: Significant business impact if unavailable
  • Medium: Limited business impact
  • Low: Minimal impact, alternatives available
Review Schedule
  • Critical Vendors: Quarterly review
  • High Risk: Semi-annual review
  • Medium/Low Risk: Annual review

Vendor Due Diligence Process

Onboarding Requirements
  1. Security questionnaire completion
  2. SOC 2 Type II or equivalent audit report review
  3. Data processing agreement (DPA) review and execution
  4. Business associate agreement (BAA) if handling PHI
  5. Security incident notification requirements
Ongoing Monitoring
  • Annual compliance certification review
  • Security incident monitoring and response
  • Service level agreement (SLA) monitoring
  • Risk reassessment based on changes in service or data access
