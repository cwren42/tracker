Management Conclusions & Sign-Off

Review Conclusions

Based on this comprehensive review of the information security risk assessment, management concludes:

  • Risk Identification: Current risk register is comprehensive and covers identified threats to information assets and business operations.
  • Risk Treatment: Appropriate risk treatment strategies have been defined and assigned to risk owners.
  • Control Effectiveness: Implemented controls are operating as designed to mitigate identified risks.
  • Incident Response: The organization maintains effective incident detection, response, and recovery capabilities.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Risk assessments are subject to ongoing review and updates based on changing conditions.
Action Items
  • Continue quarterly risk assessment reviews
  • Update risk register as new threats emerge
  • Ensure all risk controls maintain "IN PLACE" status
  • Track incident trends to identify new risk patterns
  • Maintain documentation for audit and compliance requirements
