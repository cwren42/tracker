About Maintenance Windows

Maintenance windows define specific time periods when:

  • Automatic patching and updates are allowed
  • System reboots can be performed
  • Monitoring alerts can be suppressed to reduce noise
  • Scheduled maintenance activities are preferred

Best Practices:

  • Production Servers: Schedule during off-peak hours (e.g., Sunday 2:00 AM - 6:00 AM)
  • Development/Test: More flexible windows during business hours
  • Workstations: Evening or weekend windows when users are offline
  • Critical Systems: Require manual approval even during windows
