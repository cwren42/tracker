If something looks wrong, report it. You will never be criticised for reporting something that turns out to be harmless — we would much rather look at ten false alarms than miss one real problem.

Report it if you see…
  • An email asking you to log in, pay something, or open an unexpected attachment
  • A message that looks like it is from a colleague or an executive but feels off
  • You clicked a link or entered a password somewhere you now doubt
  • A lost or stolen laptop, phone or badge
  • Someone asking for information or access they should not need
  • A device behaving strangely — unexpected pop-ups, files you did not change
  • Anything else that just does not look right
If it is urgent
Send this form and then contact IT directly so someone picks it up immediately. Do not wait for a reply if you believe data or an account is actively at risk.
