Unknown-Device Lockout

When ON, any new unrecognized device on a non-guest network (a wall-jack plug-in or corporate Wi-Fi) is auto-blocked at the controller on sight and alerts you. Guest Wi-Fi is never blocked, and existing devices are left alone. Use during visitor days / lockdowns; leave OFF for normal operation so staff devices aren't caught.
